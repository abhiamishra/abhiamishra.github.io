Artic

Welcome to Artic - a memoir for your music memories!

Click here to make your Artic

Artic is a way for you to capture the memories you made from a concert and share it with your friends.

Here is what an Artic looks like:


An Artic contains 6 simple components:

  • A place for an image to be inserted: either yours or the album cover of your artist
  • Your Section Number
  • Name of the Tour
  • Name of the Artist
  • Date
  • Venue

Each Artic is unique meaning no one's Artic will look the same - even with the same artist and venue and concert!

The best part is the creativity of an Artic is ... in your hands! Let's see how you make an Artic.

Creating an Artic

Click here for the Artic website


When you come to the Artic website, you'll see a simple layout.

On the left hand side, fill out your show details and click the "Generate Blank Artic".

This creates an Artic with out an image. You can download this image to add your own image or add an album cover to it - as we'll see shortly.


Once the blank Artic is made, simply go back to the screen and add the album cover you want the Artic to become like:


Then, click Generate Artic with Album and you'll get a customized Artic that will take the color, styles, and effects of the album you selected!


Privacy Policy

At Artic, we believe in Privacy By Design.

That's why we have the following:

  • No Image Storage: We do not host or store the images you upload or generate. Images are processed in-memory and delivered directly to your session.
